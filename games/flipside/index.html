HOW TO PLAY
I. Pick up to 5 coins and TOSS them. Every coin that lands Heads pays Chips · every Tails pays Mult. Your toss scores Chips × Mult.
II. The pattern sets the base: all heads is MIDAS, all tails NOCTURNE, alternating PENDULUM, palindromes MIRROR… Level them up with Omens.
III. Click a landed coin to REFLIP it — a shared pool each round. Or KEEP a coin: it skips this toss, then leads your next toss, face locked, ×1.5.
IV. Beat the target in 4 tosses, shop, repeat — or SKIP a Small/Big bet for a Tag. Survive 8 Antes. The House is waiting. New Pouches and Stakes unlock as you play.
